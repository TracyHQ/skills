# Worked welcomes

One conversation, once per person, and there is no second first impression. Each shape below shows
what lands and what fails while sounding friendlier.

---

## The site's copy has arrived

> Hi — you are on **joomlart.com**, and its local copy is here: the last read finished on 14 August.
>
> What were you brought in to do? If you are not sure yet, I can walk you round what is here.

**Why it passes:** it names the site, states whether the copy landed and when, and stops. The second
beat hands over instead of proposing.

**The failing version:** *"Welcome aboard! You've been brought in to help improve the site's SEO —
let's start by looking at the meta descriptions."* Nobody typed that. The assignment is invented, and
a new person has no way to know it was invented, so they may go and do it.

---

## Nothing has been synced yet

> Hi — you are on **joomlart.com**. The local copy has not arrived yet, so there is nothing here to
> read about the site itself.
>
> What were you brought in to do?

**Why it passes:** the absence is stated as a fact about the workspace, and it does not pretend to
know the site.

**The failing version:** *"The workspace looks empty, so this must be a new site."* An empty
workspace means nobody has read the site here. The site may be twenty years old.

---

## They ask what tools they have

**The failing version:** listing the servers, skills and tools. The welcome screen has already shown
them, read from the seat book. Reciting it here says it twice, and says it from
`AgentSetting/mcp` — a generated export with the team's disabled servers already stripped out, so
the second telling can be quietly wrong where the first was right.

**What passes:** *"Everything that came with your invitation is on the welcome screen. If something
you expected is missing, tell me and I will look."*

---

## The person asking is the one who sent the invitation

> **"I just added my colleague to the team."**

**The failing version:** welcoming them. They are not new here, and a welcome reads as the agent
having lost track of who it is talking to.

**What passes:** answer what they actually asked. This skill is for the newcomer's own first
conversation.
